# SAMA Counter-Fraud, Financial Crime & Biometric AI Directive
**Source Identifier**: `SAMA_ANTI_FRAUD_AI`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2024.1  
**Category**: Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Real-Time Transaction Monitoring & AI Anomaly Detection

### Clause 1.1: Mandatory Sub-Second AI Fraud Scoring
Financial entities and payment service providers operating instant payment systems (sarie, mada, Apple Pay) must deploy automated, real-time AI fraud detection engines capable of calculating risk scores on financial transactions in under 200 milliseconds prior to settlement authorization.

### Clause 1.2: Adaptive Learning & Behavioral Pattern Analysis
AI models for fraud prevention must analyze multidimensional telemetry (device fingerprinting, IP geolocation, typing cadence, behavioral biometrics, and historical velocity). Machine learning pipelines must be updated continuously to detect emerging financial crime vectors without introducing false-positive rejection spikes for legitimate citizen transactions.

---

## Section 2: Biometric Verification & Liveness Detection Standards

### Clause 2.1: Anti-Spoofing & Deepfake Liveness Detection (PAD Level 2)
AI-based biometric verification systems (facial recognition, voice biometrics) utilized during customer onboarding or high-value fund transfers must comply with ISO/IEC 30107-3 Presentation Attack Detection (PAD) Level 2 standards. AI models must actively identify and reject synthetic deepfakes, 3D masks, generative video injections, and replay attacks.

### Clause 2.2: Dual-Channel Verification for High-Risk Behavioral Anomalies
When an AI fraud scoring engine detects a high-risk anomaly (Risk Score > 85/100 or unusual cross-border velocity), the system must automatically enforce multi-factor step-up authentication or route the transaction to a specialized fraud analyst queue before funds are released.

---

## Section 3: False Positive Governance & Customer Recourse

### Clause 3.1: Human Oversight on Autonomous Account Freezing
Automated AI systems shall not permanently lock, terminate, or report a citizen or enterprise bank account to the financial intelligence unit without explicit review and signed authorization by a licensed Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance Officer.

### Clause 3.2: Rapid Redress and Dispute Resolution
Financial institutions must establish an automated grievance escalation path for customers whose transactions are erroneously flagged or blocked by AI fraud models, ensuring dispute investigation and resolution within a maximum timeframe of twenty-four (24) hours.
