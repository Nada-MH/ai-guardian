# UK FCA & Bank of England PRA Supervisory Approach to AI in Financial Services
**Source Identifier**: `UK_FCA_PRA_AI_SUPERVISION`  
**Publishing Body**: Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) & Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA)  
**Jurisdiction**: United Kingdom / International Financial Sector  
**Version**: DP5/22 & Supervisory Update 2024  
**Category**: Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Senior Management Accountability & Governance

### Clause 1.1: Allocation of AI Responsibility under the Senior Managers Regime (SM&CR)
Regulated financial firms must explicitly assign regulatory responsibility for AI models and automated decision-making systems to a designated Senior Management Function (SMF) holder under the Senior Managers and Certification Regime (SM&CR). Failure to govern AI models effectively attracts direct personal regulatory liability for the designated executive.

### Clause 1.2: Board-Level AI Competency and Oversight
Boards of Directors must possess adequate technical literacy and governance structures to critically challenge AI strategies, understand model limitations, and oversee AI risk appetite within the firm.

---

## Section 2: Consumer Duty, Fairness & Market Integrity

### Clause 2.1: Enforcement of the Consumer Duty in AI Outcomes
Under the FCA Consumer Duty (Principle 12), financial AI systems (including dynamic pricing, underwriting, and collections) must actively deliver good outcomes for retail customers, preventing foreseeable harm, predatory pricing, or unjust exclusion of vulnerable consumers.

### Clause 2.2: Algorithmic Feedback Loops and Market Stability
Firms deploying algorithmic execution or market-making models must maintain comprehensive circuit breakers and monitor cross-firm interactions to prevent algorithmic feedback loops that compromise wholesale market integrity.
