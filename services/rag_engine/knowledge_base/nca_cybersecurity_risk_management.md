# NCA Cybersecurity Risk Management & Telework Controls (TCC-1:2020)
**Source Identifier**: `NCA_RISK_MGMT_TCC`  
**Publishing Body**: National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: TCC-1:2020 & Risk Standards  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Cybersecurity  

---

## Section 1: Cybersecurity Risk Assessment & Threat Modeling

### Clause 1.1: Annual Formal Cyber Risk Assessment
Entities must conduct an annual comprehensive cybersecurity risk assessment covering all information assets, software supply chain dependencies, machine learning pipelines, and cloud instances, establishing risk treatment plans for all findings exceeding the organizational risk tolerance threshold.

### Clause 1.2: Threat Intelligence Integration with CERT.SA
Organizations must integrate indicators of compromise (IoCs) and threat intelligence feeds provided by the National Cybersecurity Center (CERT.SA) into automated firewall and endpoint detection and response (EDR) rules within four (4) hours of publication.

---

## Section 2: Secure Telework & Third-Party Vendor Risk

### Clause 2.1: Remote Access Encryption & Endpoint Posture Validation
Remote employee and contractor access to organizational systems must mandate corporate-managed encrypted laptops, device health attestation (NAC), full-disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault), and encrypted VPN/ZTNA tunnels.

### Clause 2.2: Third-Party Cybersecurity Attestation
Prior to granting any third-party vendor, external consultant, or outsourced software developer network access, the entity must obtain formal certification of compliance with NCA ECC-1:2018 or an accredited ISO 27001 audit report.
