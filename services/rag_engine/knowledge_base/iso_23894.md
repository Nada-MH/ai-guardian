# ISO/IEC 23894:2023 Information Technology - Artificial Intelligence - Guidance on Risk Management
**Source Identifier**: `ISO_IEC_23894`  
**Publishing Body**: International Organization for Standardization (ISO / IEC)  
**Version**: 2023.02  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Clause 5: AI Risk Management Principles

### Clause 5.1: Integrated AI Risk Management
AI risk management shall be an integral part of all organizational activities and decision making, including planning, management, and reporting processes related to AI system development and deployment.

### Clause 5.2: Structured and Comprehensive Approach
AI risk identification and treatment shall follow a structured, systematic approach that considers the technical, operational, ethical, and societal dimensions of AI system risks.

---

## Clause 6: AI Risk Management Framework

### Clause 6.1: Leadership Commitment to AI Risk
Top management shall demonstrate leadership and commitment with respect to AI risk management by ensuring integration with organizational governance, defining risk appetite for AI deployments, and allocating adequate resources.

### Clause 6.2: AI Risk Assessment Scope
The organization shall define the scope of AI risk management activities, considering the AI system lifecycle, intended purpose, operational environment, and affected stakeholders.

---

## Clause 7: AI Risk Assessment Process

### Clause 7.1: AI Risk Identification
The organization shall identify AI-specific risks including data quality risks, algorithmic bias, model uncertainty, lack of explainability, security vulnerabilities, privacy intrusion, and unintended behavioral outcomes.

### Clause 7.2: AI Risk Analysis
Identified AI risks shall be analyzed by examining the likelihood of occurrence and potential consequences, considering cascading effects, systemic risk propagation, and the reversibility of AI decisions.

### Clause 7.3: AI Risk Evaluation
The organization shall evaluate analyzed risks against established risk criteria and risk appetite to determine their significance and prioritize risk treatment actions.

---

## Clause 8: AI Risk Treatment

### Clause 8.1: Risk Treatment Options
Risk treatment options for AI systems include risk avoidance (not deploying), risk reduction (adding controls, explainability layers, human oversight), risk sharing (insurance, third-party validation), and risk retention (accepting residual risk with documented justification).

### Clause 8.2: Monitoring and Review
The organization shall continuously monitor and periodically review AI risk management processes, controls, and residual risk levels to ensure effectiveness and adaptation to evolving threats and regulatory changes.
