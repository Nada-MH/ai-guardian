# SAMA Internal Audit Framework for Financial Institutions
**Source Identifier**: `SAMA_INTERNAL_AUDIT_FRAMEWORK`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2022.1  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Independence, Charter & Dual Reporting Structure

### Clause 1.1: Direct Reporting Line to the Board Audit Committee
The Chief Audit Executive (CAE) must report functionally to the Board Audit Committee (BAC) and administratively to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO). The Internal Audit Charter, annual audit plan, budget, and CAE performance evaluation must be approved exclusively by the Board Audit Committee.

### Clause 1.2: Independent Audit Coverage of AI and Model Risk
The internal audit department must possess or engage qualified specialized technical competencies to independently audit automated decision-making engines, AI model risk management lifecycles, vector database security, and algorithmic fairness testing.

---

## Section 2: Audit Quality, Continuous Monitoring & Tracking

### Clause 2.1: Risk-Based Internal Audit (RBIA) Annual Planning
Internal Audit must formulate an annual Risk-Based Internal Audit (RBIA) plan covering high-risk business units, cybersecurity controls, regulatory compliance functions, and automated IT infrastructure on a minimum 12-to-24 month cyclical frequency.

### Clause 2.2: Tracking and Closure of Audit Findings
Management action plans addressing High and Critical internal audit findings must be remediated within ninety (90) calendar days. Internal Audit must independently re-test and verify technical evidence before formally closing any finding in the audit tracking register.
