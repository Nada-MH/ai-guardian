# Saudi Ministry of Commerce (MoC) New Companies Law Corporate Governance Framework
**Source Identifier**: `SAUDI_NEW_COMPANIES_LAW`  
**Publishing Body**: Ministry of Commerce (MoC)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: Royal Decree M/132 (2023)  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Corporate Law  

---

## Section 1: Corporate Fiduciary Duties, Managerial Liability & Resolutions

### Clause 1.1: Personal Liability of Directors for Ultra Vires and Gross Negligence
Under Articles 26-29 of the New Companies Law, board members and managers are personally and jointly liable for damages sustained by the company, shareholders, or third parties resulting from violation of the law, breach of the Articles of Association, gross negligence, or fraudulent acts.

### Clause 1.2: Modernized Corporate Structures & Simplified Joint Stock Companies (SJSC)
The law recognizes flexible corporate forms, including the Simplified Joint Stock Company (SJSC) and Single-Shareholder LLCs, enabling modern governance charters, multiple classes of shares, drag-along/tag-along shareholder agreements, and electronic General Assembly deliberations.

## Section 2: Statutory Reserves, Auditing & Dissolution Protections

### Clause 2.1: Elimination of Mandatory Statutory Reserve Ceiling & Flexible Retained Earnings
Companies have full statutory flexibility to establish discretionary reserve policies aligned with business growth or risk capitalization, subject to General Assembly approval.

### Clause 2.2: Protection Against Automatic Dissolution on Losses
Where a company's losses reach 50% or more of its paid-up capital, the Board must invite the Extraordinary General Assembly within sixty (60) days to adopt corrective remedial measures, eliminating automatic corporate dissolution and establishing structured restructuring lifecycles.
