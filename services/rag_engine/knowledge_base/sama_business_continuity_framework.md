# SAMA Business Continuity Management (BCM) Framework for Financial Institutions
**Source Identifier**: `SAMA_BCM_FRAMEWORK`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2022.1  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Business Impact Analysis (BIA) & Recovery Objectives

### Clause 1.1: Mandatory Sub-2-Hour Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
Financial institutions, payment system operators, and core banking infrastructure must conduct an annual Business Impact Analysis (BIA) across all critical services. Critical payment processing systems (sarie, mada, SWIFT gateways, ATM switches) must achieve a maximum Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of less than two (2) hours and a Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of zero data loss (RPO = 0).

### Clause 1.2: Active-Active Geographically Separated Disaster Recovery Sites
Tier 1 banks and critical market financial infrastructures must maintain synchronous, geographically dispersed secondary disaster recovery (DR) data centers located at least 50 kilometers away from the primary data center, with automated failover capabilities and continuous data replication.

---

## Section 2: Crisis Management, Testing & Annual Live Drills

### Clause 2.1: Annual Live Disaster Recovery Shift Drills
Financial institutions must conduct a mandatory annual unannounced live operational shift to the secondary disaster recovery site during peak transaction hours, validating transaction processing, automated AI decisioning systems, customer channel resilience, and core banking database integrity.

### Clause 2.2: Mandatory Incident Notification within 60 Minutes
In the event of an operational disruption, major cyber incident, or systemic AI service failure affecting customer banking channels for more than fifteen (15) minutes, the institution must notify SAMA's Supervisory Control Unit within sixty (60) minutes of initial detection.
