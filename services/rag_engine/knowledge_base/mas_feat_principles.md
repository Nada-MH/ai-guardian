# MAS FEAT Principles for AI/ML in Financial Services

**Source Identifier**: `MAS_FEAT_2018`  
**Publishing Body**: Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Category**: Finance Regulatory Framework  

---

### Section 1.1 — Fairness in AI Decisioning
Financial institutions deploying AI/ML models for credit scoring, underwriting, or pricing must ensure that data and algorithms do not discriminate against individuals or groups based on protected characteristics unless justified by objective actuarial data.

### Section 2.1 — Ethics & Alignment
AI systems must be aligned with ethical standards and institutional values. Financial decisions produced by algorithms must be evaluated for unintended societal harm and adverse customer outcomes.

### Section 3.1 — Accountability & Senior Management Governance
Senior management and board members retain ultimate accountability for AI model outputs. Financial institutions must assign named individual responsibility for model performance, risk management, and regulatory compliance.

### Section 4.1 — Transparency & Customer Explainability
Customers must be informed when AI systems make or materially contribute to decisions affecting financial access or credit terms. Clear, understandable explanations of key decision factors must be provided upon request.
