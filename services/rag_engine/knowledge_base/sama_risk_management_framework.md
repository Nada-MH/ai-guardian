# SAMA Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Framework for Financial Institutions
**Source Identifier**: `SAMA_ERM_FRAMEWORK`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2023.1  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) & Risk Governance

### Clause 1.1: Board-Approved Quantitative Risk Appetite Limits
The Board of Directors must annually approve an Enterprise Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) establishing qualitative declarations and quantitative threshold limits across credit risk, market risk, operational risk, cyber risk, liquidity risk, and artificial intelligence model risk.

### Clause 1.2: Independent Chief Risk Officer (CRO) Authority
The Chief Risk Officer (CRO) must operate with direct, uninhibited access to the Board Risk Committee and Board of Directors, possessing explicit executive veto authority over new product launches, high-risk credit underwriting deviations, and unvalidated AI algorithm deployments.

## Section 2: Capital & Liquidity Adequacy Assessment (ICAAP & ILAAP)

### Clause 2.1: Annual Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP)
Financial institutions must submit an annual ICAAP report to SAMA evaluating Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 capital adequacy under baseline and multi-year severe economic stress scenarios (including macroeconomic contraction, oil price shocks, real estate asset deflation, and high-impact cyber losses).

### Clause 2.2: Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process (ILAAP)
Banks must maintain robust liquidity buffers complying with Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR > 100%) and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR > 100%) under continuous 30-day liquidity stress assumptions.
