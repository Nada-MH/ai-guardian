# SAMA Consumer Protection Principles & Responsible Lending Regulations
**Source Identifier**: `SAMA_CONSUMER_PROTECTION`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2023.1  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Responsible Financing & Debt Burden Ratios (DBR)

### Clause 1.1: Maximum Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) Limits
Financial institutions granting retail consumer loans, credit cards, or personal financing must strictly enforce statutory Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) ceilings:
- Maximum 33.33% of monthly net salary for regular retail borrowers.
- Maximum 45.0% for high-income earners with verified monthly salary exceeding SAR 25,000.
Automated credit scoring models and AI decisioning engines are legally prohibited from approving financing exceeding these statutory ratios.

### Clause 1.2: Standardized Annual Percentage Rate (APR) Disclosures
Financing entities must present customers with a standardized Key Facts Statement (KFS) explicitly disclosing the full Annual Percentage Rate (APR), total cost of credit, administrative fees, early settlement schedule, and default penalties prior to contract execution.

## Section 2: Fair Treatment, Transparency & Complaint Resolution

### Clause 2.1: Prohibition of Unfair and Deceptive Practices
Financial entities are prohibited from using deceptive marketing, hidden fees, complex contract clauses, or algorithmic price gouging. Loan interest rates and terms must remain transparent and non-discriminatory across protected demographic groups.

### Clause 2.2: Mandatory 10-Business-Day Complaint Resolution Standard
Financial institutions must acknowledge customer complaints within twenty-four (24) hours and provide a formal, written resolution or escalation response within a maximum of ten (10) business days. Unresolved complaints automatically escalate to SAMA's Consumer Protection Portal (Samacares).
