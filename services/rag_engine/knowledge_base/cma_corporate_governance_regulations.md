# Saudi Capital Market Authority (CMA) Corporate Governance Regulations
**Source Identifier**: `CMA_CORPORATE_GOVERNANCE_REGS`  
**Publishing Body**: Capital Market Authority (CMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2023.2 (Amended Resolution No. 8-16-2023)  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Corporate Governance  

---

## Section 1: Shareholder Rights, General Assembly & Board Duties

### Clause 1.1: Protection of Shareholder Rights and Equitable Treatment
Listed joint-stock companies in KSA must guarantee fundamental shareholder rights, including voting rights on a one-share-one-vote basis, timely distribution of dividends, access to corporate disclosures, and electronic voting via Tadawulaty for all General Assembly meetings.

### Clause 1.2: Fiduciary Duties of Directors (Duty of Care & Duty of Loyalty)
Members of the Board of Directors must exercise their duties with the skill, diligence, and care of a prudent person, placing the corporate entity's long-term interests above personal gain and avoiding conflicts of interest.

---

## Section 2: Related Party Transactions & Insider Trading Controls

### Clause 2.1: Prior Approval for Related Party Transactions
Any business transaction or contract in which a board member or senior executive has a direct or indirect interest must be approved in advance by the Ordinary General Assembly. The interested board member is legally barred from voting on the resolution.

### Clause 2.2: Prohibition of Insider Trading & Blackout Periods
Board members, executives, and insiders possess an absolute prohibition against trading in the company's shares during mandatory statutory blackout periods (e.g. 15 days prior to quarter-end financial releases and 30 days prior to annual results announcements).
