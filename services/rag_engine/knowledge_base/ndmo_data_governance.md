# NDMO National Data Governance Framework
**Source Identifier**: `NDMO_DATA_GOV`  
**Publishing Body**: National Data Management Office (NDMO)  
**Version**: 2.1 (2023)  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Domain 1: Data Management and Strategy

### Clause 1.1: National Data Classification
All data processed by AI systems in government and regulated entities within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia must be classified according to NDMO data classification levels: Public, Restricted, Confidential, and Top Secret. Classification drives security controls, access policies, and cross-border transfer rules.

### Clause 1.2: Data Stewardship and Ownership
Organizations must assign Data Stewards responsible for the quality, accuracy, and lifecycle management of datasets used in AI training and inference, including regular audits of data lineage and provenance.

---

## Domain 2: Data Quality and Integrity

### Clause 2.1: Data Quality Standards for AI Training
Datasets used to train financial AI models must adhere to defined quality standards including accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and validity. Quality assessments must be documented and repeated at defined intervals.

### Clause 2.2: Data Lineage and Provenance Tracking
Organizations must maintain traceable data lineage records documenting the origin, transformation history, and ownership chain of all datasets used in AI model development, enabling audit and reproducibility.

---

## Domain 3: Data Sharing and Interoperability

### Clause 3.1: Data Sharing Agreements for AI
Data shared between organizational units or with third-party AI vendors must be governed by formal data sharing agreements specifying purpose limitation, retention periods, security requirements, and permitted AI use cases.

### Clause 3.2: Open Data and API Standards
Where applicable, organizations should publish non-sensitive datasets and AI model outputs using interoperable formats and standardized APIs to promote transparency, innovation, and regulatory supervision.

---

## Domain 4: Data Protection and Privacy

### Clause 4.1: Alignment with Saudi PDPL
All data governance practices must ensure compliance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), including data subject consent management, purpose limitation, and the right to erasure for personal data processed by AI systems.

### Clause 4.2: De-identification and Anonymization
Personal data used for AI model training must be de-identified or anonymized using industry-standard techniques (e.g., k-anonymity, differential privacy) wherever possible, with documented risk assessments for re-identification.
