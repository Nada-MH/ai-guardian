# SDAIA Generative AI & Large Language Model Guidelines

**Source Identifier**: `SDAIA_GENAI_2024`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA)  
**Version**: 2024.1  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

### Section 1.1 — Generative Content Watermarking & Attribution
Organizations deploying Generative AI models or LLMs must apply cryptographic digital watermarking and provenance metadata to all synthetic outputs (text, audio, image, code) to prevent misinformation and enable public verification.

### Section 2.1 — Hallucination Mitigation & Verification
Generative AI systems deployed in high-impact domains (finance, healthcare, legal) must implement automated RAG verification and ground truth checking pipelines to eliminate hallucinations before outputs reach end users.

### Section 3.1 — Prompt Injection & Adversarial Security
Generative AI APIs and chat interfaces must incorporate real-time prompt firewalling, input sanitization, and output filtering to defend against prompt injection, jailbreaking, and system prompt exfiltration.

### Section 4.1 — IP & Copyright Data Governance
Training datasets and RAG retrieval stores used by Generative AI models must respect intellectual property rights, Saudi data protection rules (PDPL), and maintain audit trails of all ingested external documents.
