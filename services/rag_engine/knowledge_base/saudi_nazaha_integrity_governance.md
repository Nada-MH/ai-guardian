# Saudi Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority (Nazaha) Corporate Integrity & Whistleblower Standard

**Publishing Body**: Oversight and Anti-Corruption Authority (Nazaha)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2023.1  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Corporate Integrity  

---

## Section 1: Anti-Corruption Internal Controls & Conflict of Interest

### Clause 1.1: Mandatory Corporate Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy
All government contractors, banks, publicly listed corporations, and large enterprise entities must adopt a formal Board-approved Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption policy, establishing a zero-tolerance stance on illegal facilitation payments, kickbacks, and extortion.

### Clause 1.2: Annual Executive Financial and Asset Conflict Disclosure
Directors, procurement officers, credit committee members, and executives must execute annual conflict of interest disclosures declaring outside commercial interests, board memberships, family ties to corporate vendors, and substantial equity holdings.

---

## Section 2: Whistleblower Protection & Investigative Integrity

### Clause 2.1: Anonymous Whistleblower Hotline and Non-Retaliation Guarantee
Entities must provide secure, independent, and anonymous reporting channels (hotline, encrypted web portal) enabling employees and external partners to report financial fraud, ethical misconduct, or regulatory non-compliance without fear of retaliation, termination, or discrimination.

### Clause 2.2: Mandatory Reporting to Nazaha upon Uncovering Criminal Fraud
Upon uncovering credible evidence of bribery, embezzlement of public funds, procurement bid-rigging, or money laundering, the organization must notify Nazaha and relevant judicial authorities within twenty-four (24) hours, preserving all electronic evidence, transaction logs, and internal communications.
