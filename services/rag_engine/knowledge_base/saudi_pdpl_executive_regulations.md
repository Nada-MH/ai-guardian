# Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) Executive Regulations & Cross-Border Data Transfer
**Source Identifier**: `SAUDI_PDPL_EXEC_REGS_2024`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2024.1 (Cabinet Resolution No. 43)  
**Category**: Core AI Governance / Law  

---

## Section 1: Automated Processing, Profiling & Individual Consent

### Clause 1.1: Mandatory Right to Object to Solely Automated Decision-Making
Under Article 29 of the PDPL Executive Regulations, data subjects have the legally protected right not to be subjected to decisions based solely on automated processing or AI profiling that produce legal consequences or significantly affect their rights, financial status, or personal liberty, unless explicitly authorized by law or based on explicit informed consent.

### Clause 1.2: Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for AI Deployments
Entities must conduct and formally document a comprehensive Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) prior to deploying AI or machine learning models that process personal data at scale, utilize automated facial/biometric identification, or execute credit profiling.

## Section 2: Cross-Border Data Transfer Framework for AI Systems

### Clause 2.1: Conditions for Cross-Border AI Data Transfers
Personal data processed in KSA may only be transferred outside the Kingdom for AI model training or inference if:
1. The destination jurisdiction has been declared an Adequate Jurisdiction by SDAIA; OR
2. The controller implements Appropriate Safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses - SCCs approved by SDAIA, or Binding Corporate Rules - BCRs); AND
3. The transfer does not impair national security, vital sovereign interests, or violate in-Kingdom localization mandates.

### Clause 2.2: Data Minimization & 72-Hour Breach Notification
AI data processing pipelines must enforce rigorous data minimization and tokenization. In the event of a security compromise or unauthorized data exfiltration affecting personal data within AI databases, the controller must notify SDAIA and affected data subjects within seventy-two (72) hours.
