# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI RMF 1.0)

**Source Identifier**: `NIST_AI_RMF_1_0`  
**Publishing Body**: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

### Section 1.1 — Govern Core Function
Governance structures, processes, and personnel responsibilities must be defined to identify, assess, manage, and monitor AI risks. AI risk management policies must be aligned with legal requirements, institutional values, and organizational risk tolerance thresholds.

### Section 2.1 — Map Function Context & Categorization
Organizations must map AI system capabilities, intended purpose, context of use, and potential negative impacts across demographic groups. System categorization must document dependencies on third-party components, data sources, and algorithmic models.

### Section 3.1 — Measure Function Quantitative Testing
AI systems must undergo rigorous, continuous measurement of performance, accuracy, fairness, explainability, and security metrics. Quantitative evaluations must include bias auditing across protected attributes and stress-testing under adversarial conditions.

### Section 4.1 — Manage Function Risk Treatment
Identified AI risks must be mitigated through prioritized risk response plans. System failures, unexpected outputs, or performance degradation must trigger automated incident response, human intervention, or emergency fallback protocols.
