# HKMA Principles on AI in Banking

**Source Identifier**: `HKMA_AI_BANKING_2023`  
**Publishing Body**: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)  
**Version**: 2023.1  
**Category**: Finance Regulatory Framework  

---

### Section 1.1 — Governance & Board Oversight
Authorized institutions deploying AI must establish an effective model governance framework with clear board oversight, independent model validation, and ongoing audit logging of automated decision systems.

### Section 2.1 — Model Validation & Backtesting
AI models used in credit underwriting and risk assessment must undergo rigorous pre-deployment backtesting against historical economic stress scenarios and periodic post-deployment re-validation.

### Section 3.1 — Consumer Protection & Transparency
Banks must provide clear disclosures to consumers regarding the use of AI in credit decisions, including key factors influencing adverse outcomes and formal dispute resolution pathways.
