# ISO/IEC 22989 & ISO/IEC 24028 — AI Trustworthiness, Concepts & Robustness
**Source Identifier**: `ISO_IEC_22989_24028`  
**Publishing Body**: International Organization for Standardization (ISO) / International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC)  
**Jurisdiction**: International / Global  
**Version**: 2022 / 2020 Joint Standards  
**Category**: Core AI Governance / Standards  

---

## Section 1: AI Trustworthiness Framework & Threat Modeling

### Clause 1.1: Multi-Dimensional Trustworthiness Attributes
AI systems must be engineered and evaluated across key trustworthiness dimensions: accuracy, reliability, resilience, robustness, safety, security, privacy, explainability, transparency, fairness, and accountability.

### Clause 1.2: Adversarial Attack Defense & Robustness Testing
Organizations must perform structured threat modeling against known AI vulnerabilities, specifically:
- Data Poisoning attacks targeting training or fine-tuning pipelines.
- Adversarial Evasion attacks using imperceptible input perturbations.
- Model Inversion and Membership Inference attacks attempting to extract confidential training data.

---

## Section 2: Lifecycle Quality Management & Continuous Verification

### Clause 2.1: Lifecycle Stage Gate Controls
AI systems must pass formal quality gates at each lifecycle stage: Inception, Design, Data Engineering, Model Building, Verification & Validation, Deployment, Operational Monitoring, and Retirement.

### Clause 2.2: Continuous Robustness & Concept Drift Telemetry
AI models in production must maintain continuous automated monitoring of prediction distributions, feature drift metrics, and confidence calibration scores to detect environmental distribution shifts.
