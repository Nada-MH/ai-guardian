# Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) & AI Governance
**Source Identifier**: `SAUDI_PDPL`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA)  
**Version**: Royal Decree M/19  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Article 4: Data Processing Principles and Lawful Basis
Personal data processing by AI systems must be conducted lawfully, fairly, and transparently based on explicit consent or statutory authorization under Saudi Arabian law.

## Article 13: Data Minimization in Training Data
AI model training pipelines must enforce strict data minimization, ensuring that only personal data directly relevant and strictly necessary for the designated financial purpose is processed.

## Article 24: Cross-Border Data Transfer Restrictions
Personal financial data processed by AI systems shall not be transferred outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia unless explicit approval from SDAIA is granted and adequate security safeguards are verified.

## Article 27: Automated Processing & Profiling Rights
Data subjects have the right to request human intervention and challenge automated decisions produced by AI algorithms that negatively affect their financial rights or legal status.
