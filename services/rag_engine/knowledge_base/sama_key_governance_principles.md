# SAMA Key Principles of Governance in Financial Institutions
**Source Identifier**: `SAMA_KEY_GOVERNANCE_PRINCIPLES`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2021/2024 Update  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Board Structure, Composition and Responsibilities

### Clause 1.1: Board Composition and Independent Director Mandates
The Board of Directors of every financial institution operating in KSA must comprise at least nine (9) and not more than eleven (11) members, with independent directors representing at least one-third (1/3) of the board. The Chairman of the Board must be an independent non-executive director and cannot simultaneously hold the position of Chief Executive Officer (CEO) or any executive management role.

### Clause 1.2: Mandatory Board Committees (Audit, Risk, Nomination & Remuneration)
The Board must establish dedicated standing committees:
1. **Board Audit Committee (BAC)**: Composed exclusively of non-executive and independent directors, with at least one member possessing recognized expert qualifications in accounting and financial reporting.
2. **Board Risk Committee (BRC)**: Responsible for approving the Enterprise Risk Appetite Statement (RAS), overseeing capital and liquidity risks, and supervising AI and model risk governance.
3. **Nomination & Remuneration Committee (NRC)**: Responsible for Fit and Proper vetting and annual executive compensation reviews aligned with long-term risk performance.

## Section 2: Three Lines of Defense (3LoD) Governance Model

### Clause 2.1: Operational Independence of Control Functions
Financial institutions must enforce an uncompromising Three Lines of Defense (3LoD) organizational model:
- **First Line (Business & IT Units)**: Directly responsible for identifying, assessing, and managing operational, credit, and algorithmic risks arising from day-to-day operations.
- **Second Line (Chief Risk Officer & Chief Compliance Officer)**: Independent monitoring, challenge, risk policy formulation, and regulatory compliance oversight.
- **Third Line (Chief Audit Executive / Internal Audit)**: Independent, objective assurance directly reporting to the Board Audit Committee.

### Clause 2.2: Fit and Proper Requirements and Conflict of Interest
All candidates for Key Persons and Senior Management positions (Directors, CEO, CRO, CCO, CAE, CFO, CISO) must obtain formal written "No-Objection" clearance from SAMA prior to appointment, demonstrating impeccable integrity, financial solvency, and technical competence.
