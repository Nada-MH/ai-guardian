# ITU AI Readiness Framework & ITU-T Y.3172
**Source Identifier**: `ITU_AI_FRAMEWORK`  
**Publishing Body**: International Telecommunication Union (ITU)  
**Version**: Y.3172 (2022)  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Section 1: AI Readiness Pillars

### Clause 1.1: Governance and Regulatory Readiness
National and organizational AI readiness requires established governance structures, regulatory frameworks, and institutional capacity to guide, monitor, and enforce responsible AI development and deployment.

### Clause 1.2: Infrastructure and Data Readiness
AI readiness depends on adequate digital infrastructure including computing resources, connectivity, data availability, and data management standards necessary to support AI system development and operation at scale.

### Clause 1.3: Human Capital and Skills Readiness
Organizations must invest in developing AI literacy and technical skills across their workforce, ensuring sufficient expertise in AI system design, operation, risk management, and ethical governance.

---

## Section 2: ITU-T Y.3172 Framework for ML in Future Networks

### Clause 2.1: ML Pipeline Lifecycle Stages
The AI/ML lifecycle shall be managed through defined stages: data collection and preparation, model training and optimization, model evaluation and validation, deployment, and continuous monitoring with feedback loops.

### Clause 2.2: Quality of Service Requirements for AI Systems
AI systems deployed in network and financial service contexts must meet defined Quality of Service (QoS) requirements including inference latency, throughput, availability, and accuracy targets, with monitoring mechanisms to detect and alert on SLA violations.

### Clause 2.3: Interoperability Standards
AI system components and interfaces should conform to open interoperability standards, enabling portability of models across platforms, reproducibility of results, and integration with enterprise governance frameworks.
