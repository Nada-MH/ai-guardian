# Basel Committee Principles for Operational Resilience and AI Model Governance
**Source Identifier**: `BASEL_AI_PRINCIPLES`  
**Publishing Body**: Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS)  
**Version**: BCBS Newsletter 2023  
**Category**: Finance Regulatory  

---

## Principle 1: Sound AI Model Governance in Banking

### Clause 1.1: Board Responsibility for AI Risk
Boards of directors of internationally active banks must exercise adequate oversight of AI-driven processes, ensuring that AI adoption aligns with the institution's risk appetite statement and strategic objectives.

### Clause 1.2: Integration into Operational Risk Management
AI model risk must be integrated into the institution's operational risk management framework, with dedicated capital allocation where AI model failures could result in material financial losses.

---

## Principle 2: Prudential Treatment of AI Models

### Clause 2.1: Model Risk Quantification
Banks using AI/ML models for regulatory capital calculations, credit risk assessment, or market risk measurement must quantify model risk through backtesting, stress testing, and scenario analysis under extreme financial conditions.

### Clause 2.2: Conservative Adjustments
Where AI model outputs are used for prudential purposes, supervisors may require banks to apply conservative adjustments or model risk add-ons to account for model uncertainty, lack of interpretability, or insufficient historical validation.

---

## Principle 3: Third-Party AI Dependencies

### Clause 3.1: Concentration Risk in AI Services
Banks must assess and manage concentration risk arising from reliance on a limited number of third-party AI service providers, cloud infrastructure platforms, or data vendors, ensuring operational continuity plans are in place.

### Clause 3.2: Outsourcing Accountability
Outsourcing of AI functions does not diminish a bank's responsibility for the safety, soundness, and regulatory compliance of its operations. Banks must retain sufficient internal expertise to oversee and challenge outsourced AI services.

---

## Principle 4: Operational Resilience of AI Infrastructure

### Clause 4.1: Business Continuity for AI-Critical Processes
Banks must identify AI systems supporting critical business services and establish tolerance levels for disruption, with tested recovery procedures ensuring resumption within defined time objectives.

### Clause 4.2: Change Management for AI Models
Deployment of new AI models or significant model updates must follow a structured change management process including impact assessment, stakeholder sign-off, staged rollout, and post-deployment monitoring.
