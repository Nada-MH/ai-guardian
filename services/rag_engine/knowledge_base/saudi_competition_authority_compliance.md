# Saudi General Authority for Competition (GAC) Anti-Monopoly & Market Compliance Standard
**Source Identifier**: `SAUDI_GAC_COMPETITION_RULES`  
**Publishing Body**: General Authority for Competition (GAC)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2023.1 (Royal Decree M/75)  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Antitrust  

---

## Section 1: Abuse of Dominant Position & Algorithmic Collusion

### Clause 1.1: Prohibition of Dominant Market Abuse & Predatory Pricing
Entities with a dominant market share (exceeding 40% of relevant market turnover) are strictly prohibited from abusing their market power by imposing predatory pricing, tying/bundling products, refusal to supply essential facilities, or creating unjust market barriers for FinTech competitors.

### Clause 1.2: Algorithmic Pricing Collusion & Information Sharing
Market participants are legally prohibited from utilizing shared AI pricing algorithms, dynamic coordination models, or centralized data platforms that facilitate horizontal price coordination, market partitioning, or bid-rigging in tenders.

---

## Section 2: Economic Concentration (Merger Control) Filings

### Clause 2.1: Mandatory Economic Concentration Notification Thresholds
Entities participating in mergers, acquisitions, or joint ventures where total worldwide annual turnover of participating parties exceeds SAR 200 Million must submit an official Economic Concentration notification to GAC at least ninety (90) days prior to transaction closing.

### Clause 2.2: Market Integrity Penalties & Remedies
Violations of competition law attract statutory fines of up to 10% of total annual turnover, revocation of commercial licenses, and mandatory disgorgement of illicit profits.
