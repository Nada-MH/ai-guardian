# Saudi Insurance Authority (IA) Comprehensive Governance & Solvency Rules
**Source Identifier**: `SAUDI_IA_GOVERNANCE_RULES`  
**Publishing Body**: Insurance Authority (IA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2024.1 (Cabinet Resolution No. 85)  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Finance Regulatory  

---

## Section 1: Insurance Governance & Actuarial Oversight

### Clause 1.1: Appointed Actuary Independence and Reserve Certification
Every licensed insurance and reinsurance company in KSA must appoint an independent, accredited Appointed Actuary approved by the Insurance Authority (IA). The actuary must independently calculate and certify quarterly technical reserves (Incurred But Not Reported - IBNR, Premium Deficiency Reserves - PDR) in compliance with IFRS 17 standards.

### Clause 1.2: AI & Algorithmic Pricing Governance in Insurance
Automated insurance underwriting, dynamic vehicle telematics pricing, and AI claims settlement models must undergo bi-annual actuarial review to ensure premium calculations are actuarially sound, non-discriminatory, and fully compliant with IA market conduct regulations.

## Section 2: Solvency Margins, Claims Settlement & Reinsurance

### Clause 2.1: Minimum Solvency Margin Maintenance
Insurance companies must maintain a minimum Solvency Margin ratio of 100% at all times, covering underwriting risk, market investment risk, counterparty credit risk, and operational risk. Falling below 100% solvency triggers mandatory capital injection plans and regulatory intervention by the Insurance Authority.

### Clause 2.2: Mandatory 15-Day Motor and Health Claims Settlement Standard
Insurers must settle straightforward motor third-party liability (TPL) and comprehensive claims within a maximum statutory period of fifteen (15) days from complete documentation submission, maintaining automated electronic status tracking accessible to policyholders.
