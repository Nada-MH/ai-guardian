# OECD Principles on Artificial Intelligence
**Source Identifier**: `OECD_AI_PRINCIPLES`  
**Publishing Body**: Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)  
**Version**: 2024.05  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Principle 1.1: Inclusive Growth, Sustainable Development, and Well-being
AI actors should proactively engage in responsible stewardship of trustworthy AI to foster inclusive growth, sustainable development, and financial well-being.

## Principle 1.2: Human-centered Values and Fairness
AI systems should be designed in a way that respects the rule of law, human rights, democratic values, and diversity, featuring appropriate safeguards against bias and systemic discrimination.

## Principle 1.3: Transparency and Explainability
AI actors should commit to transparency and responsible disclosure regarding AI systems, ensuring stakeholders understand AI-based outcomes and are enabled to challenge them.

## Principle 1.4: Robustness, Security, and Safety
AI systems should function robustly, securely, and safely throughout their operational lifecycles, equipped with continuous risk management to address adversarial threats.

## Principle 1.5: Accountability
AI actors should be accountable for the proper functioning of AI systems and for respect of the above principles, based on their roles and context.
