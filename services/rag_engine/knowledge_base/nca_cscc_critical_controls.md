# NCA Critical Systems Cybersecurity Controls (CSCC-1:2019)
**Source Identifier**: `NCA_CSCC_2019`  
**Publishing Body**: National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: CSCC-1:2019  
**Category**: Saudi GRC & Cybersecurity  

---

## Section 1: Critical National Infrastructure & Defense-in-Depth

### Clause 1.1: Identification and Isolation of Critical National Systems
Entities operating Critical Systems (CS) in Energy, Water, Transportation, Telecommunications, Government, or Financial Services must implement physical and logical network isolation, air-gapping SCADA/ICS operational networks from corporate IT networks.

### Clause 1.2: 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) Monitoring
Critical systems must be connected to a 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) equipped with automated Security Information and Event Management (SIEM), continuous behavioral anomaly detection, and automated threat containment.

---

## Section 2: Zero-Trust Architecture & Privilege Access Management

### Clause 2.1: Privileged Access Management (PAM) & Session Recording
All administrative and elevated access to critical operating systems, databases, and AI algorithms must be mediated via a dedicated Privileged Access Management (PAM) solution enforcing time-limited credential vaulting, keystroke logging, and full session video recording.

### Clause 2.2: Mandatory Critical Patching SLA within 72 Hours
Security patches categorized as "Critical" by the NCA National Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.SA) or software vendors must be tested and deployed to critical production systems within seventy-two (72) hours of release.
