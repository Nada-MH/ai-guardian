# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Artificial Intelligence - Management System (AIMS)
**Source Identifier**: `ISO_IEC_42001`  
**Publishing Body**: International Organization for Standardization (ISO / IEC)  
**Version**: 2023.12  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Clause 4: Context of the Organization

### Clause 4.1: Understanding the Organization and its Context
The organization shall determine external and internal issues relevant to its purpose that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcomes of its AI Management System (AIMS).

### Clause 4.2: Understanding Needs and Expectations of Interested Parties
The organization shall identify interested parties (regulators, customers, auditors) and their requirements relevant to the AI management system, including ethical standards and legal obligations.

---

## Clause 6: Planning and Risk Management

### Clause 6.1: Actions to Address Risks and Opportunities
The organization shall establish an AI risk assessment process to identify, analyze, and evaluate risks related to AI system design, development, deployment, and operational impact.

### Clause 6.2: AI Objectives and Planning to Achieve Them
The organization shall establish measurable AI objectives at relevant functions and levels, ensuring alignment with responsible AI principles and regulatory compliance mandates.

---

## Annex A: AI Control Objectives and Controls (Normative)

### Control A.5: Internal Policy for AI Systems
An AI policy shall be defined, approved by management, published, and communicated to employees and relevant external parties.

### Control A.6: AI System Impact Assessment
The organization shall conduct AI Impact Assessments (AIIA) prior to developing or deploying new AI systems, evaluating societal, economic, legal, and operational risks.

### Control A.7: AI System Lifecycle Governance
Controls shall be established to govern the entire AI system lifecycle, including concept, acquisition, design, development, testing, validation, deployment, operation, and decommissioning.

### Control A.8: Data for AI Systems
Data used for training, testing, and operating AI systems shall be managed for quality, provenance, relevance, completeness, and protection against bias and unauthorized access.

### Control A.9: Transparency and Explainability
The organization shall provide appropriate transparency and explainability mechanisms for AI system decisions based on stakeholder needs and risk tier classification.
