# National Strategy for Data & AI (NSDAI) Governance Mandates
**Source Identifier**: `SAUDI_NSDAI_STRATEGY`  
**Publishing Body**: Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA)  
**Jurisdiction**: Saudi Arabia (KSA)  
**Version**: 2024 (Vision 2030 Realization)  
**Category**: Core AI Governance  

---

## Section 1: Sovereign AI Capability & Data Stewardship

### Clause 1.1: National AI Ethics Alignment
All public and private entities developing or deploying AI applications within Saudi Arabia must align their technological architectures with the National Strategy for Data & AI (NSDAI) core pillars: ethical AI stewardship, data sovereignty, socio-economic value creation, and local talent development.

### Clause 1.2: Open Data Sharing & National Data Classification
Entities processing non-confidential public sector datasets must adhere to NDMO Open Data guidelines, ensuring datasets used to train public-facing AI systems are machine-readable, accurately tagged, and free of discriminatory historical biases.

---

## Section 2: AI Professional Accreditation & Continuous Capacity Building

### Clause 2.1: Certified AI Governance Officers
Organizations operating Tier 1 Critical AI infrastructure in government, healthcare, energy, or financial services must appoint certified AI Governance & Ethics Officers holding accredited professional qualifications recognized by SDAIA.

### Clause 2.2: Continuous Model Monitoring and Socio-Economic Impact Review
Entities must conduct annual reviews assessing the broader socio-economic impact of deployed automated systems on the local workforce, consumer welfare, and national digital infrastructure resilience.
